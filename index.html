About

I am an attending paediatric endocrinologist in the Endocrinology Unit, Department of Pediatrics, Faculty of Medicine, Prince of Songkla University, and care for children at Songklanagarind Hospital in Hat Yai, southern Thailand.

What I do

  • Clinical care

    Diagnosis and long-term care of children with growth, pubertal, thyroid, bone and adrenal disorders.

  • Research

    Short stature genetics, GH–IGF axis, bone health, vitamin D and adrenal function in Thai children.

  • Teaching

    Training medical students, paediatric residents and endocrine fellows at Prince of Songkla University.

  • Academic service

    Member of the Thai Society for Paediatric Endocrinology and The Endocrine Society of Thailand, with health promotion outreach to families in southern Thailand.

Current research
